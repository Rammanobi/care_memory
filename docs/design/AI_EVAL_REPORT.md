## Open gaps (known bugs, expected failures)

- **ASK-NO-OUTPUT-GUARD**: Ask-box LLM answers are only number-checked: advice or an obeyed injection with no numbers reaches the doctor (no advice/injection filter; QueryAnswer.refused unused).
- **ASK-PII-ECHO**: Ask-box canned fallback echoes raw record text, so a phone number in a note reaches the answer (the PII guard correctly blocks it from the LLM).
- **ASK-ROUTE-SUBSTR**: Ask-box canned routing matches substrings ('need' in 'need a refill', 'red' in 'registered') and sends the question to the wrong tool.
- **ASK-ROUTE-TE**: Ask-box canned routing is English-only; Telugu list questions fall to the generic answer.
- **BANNED-DRIFT**: cm/validator/banned_terms.py (narrator guard) misses lower-case 'tb' and second-line drug names that cm/router/banned.py blocks; the two lists have drifted.
- **LONG-ECE**: Baseline risk model is miscalibrated: Day-60 ECE is above 0.10 and worse than the population average (Brier and AUROC do improve).
- **NARR-CONTENT**: Narrator keeps cited sentences that give dosing advice, a diagnosis or a blaming tone; cite-or-cut checks refs and numbers only, there is no content filter.

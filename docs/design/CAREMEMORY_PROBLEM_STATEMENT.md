# CareMemory: AI Care Memory for Continuous TB Treatment Follow-Up

**PROBLEM DESCRIPTION**

Patients on long TB treatment often drop out, stay on the wrong medicines, or miss support they are entitled to. Six parties each hold only one piece of the patient's journey: the patient, the private doctor, the lab, the chemist, the treatment supporter and the Ni-kshay portal. Over the 6+ months of treatment, nobody connects these pieces.

Build a privacy-conscious care loop with these properties:
- It gathers daily inputs through channels each person opts into: Telegram, Gmail, Calendar, optional wearables, or a human treatment supporter.
- It keeps a growing AI memory for each patient that detects contradictions and early warning signs.
- Every AI-drafted action goes to a doctor, who reviews it and decides.
- It must not replace clinical judgement.

**EXPECTED OUTPUT**

- A working care-loop prototype with opt-in multi-channel check-ins and a supporter (Saathi) workflow.
- A doctor review queue.
- A patient memory timeline showing a Day 3 vs Day 60 comparison.
- Contradiction and risk flags with cited evidence.
- A district dashboard.
- A 60-day synthetic-data evaluation.

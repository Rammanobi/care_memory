fHow a patient question reaches the doctor

  1. 1 Patient askson Telegram (free text) or in the patient app
  2. 2 Acknowledgementtemplate reply, no AI text; urgent wording escalates at once
  3. 3 Triage and brieftriage plus a cited research brief and a safe suggested reply, for the doctor only
  4. 4 Notificationlive notification in the doctor console, plus a doctor email (dry-run by default)
  5. 5 Doctor decidesedits or approves the reply
  6. 6 Threaded replysent on the same channel; the patient sees waiting, then answered

Built and tested offline (Telegram: 13 tests; Gmail simulation: 55 scenarios). The full loop was verified live on 25 Oct 2026. Real Gmail inbound/push and Calendar are not verified against real Google.
